Telegram message (text)
    ↓
TelegramClient.fetchUpdates() - long-polling, 30s timeout
    ↓
handleUpdate() → handleMessage(update.message)
    ↓
Authorization check: config.allowedChatId
    ↓
If "/abort": handleAbort() - interrupts current run, disposes session
    ↓
If "/command": enqueue(chatId, () => handleCommand(chatId, text))
    ↓
If plain text: enqueue(chatId, () => runTurn(chatId, text))
    ↓
runTurn():
    - Creates ReplyStreamer(tg, chatId)
    - Calls modeFor(s).run(prompt, ui)
    ↓
AgentMode.run() → Agent.run(prompt) or Agent.continue(prompt)
OR
CoreMode.run() → ClineCore.send({ sessionId, prompt })
    ↓
Streaming deltas sent to Telegram via ReplyStreamer.stream()
    ↓
Final answer sent via ReplyStreamer.done()
